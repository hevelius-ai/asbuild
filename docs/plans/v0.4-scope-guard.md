# Three-model reviews without scope creep (v0.4.0)

> Copied from the design kit on 2026-11-15. The three design views, the review records and the proof outputs stay on the owner's computer; the proofs ran in the private repository `hevelius-ai/asbuild-proofs`.

The owner chose P1 B (three models at extra effort on every pull request) on condition that it does not end like Agile Dev Team. This is what went wrong there, what the playbook already covers, and the nine guards that close the rest.

## What happened in Agile Dev Team (archive, checked)

- One audit (2 Nov 2026, three models plus seven triage agents) gave **172 distinct findings: 60 HIGH, 105 MEDIUM, 3 LOW** (`local/designs/audit-2026-11-02-results.md`). Triage raised 16 grades and lowered none.
- The owner's rule "a fix bundle answers every finding in its scope" turned them into a redo: **58 pull requests, 685 to 890 hours**; the whole first phase 1,170 to 1,660 hours. The project was retired on 9 Nov.
- Decisions needed consensus (every voice, average 80 or more); one design reached revision 26, and story reviews ran 11, 12 and 17 rounds before a cap existed.
- Root causes: no budget or hard stop at the start; no grade for "out of scope"; reviewers briefed on the whole code, not the agreed scope; open-ended security chases; consensus as the bar; decided items reopened; facts argued instead of tested; designs ahead of the build; triage that added instead of filtered; "fix the whole class" turning each finding into a rebuild.

## Already covered by the playbook

Budget, "done" and hard stop (1.2); the PARKED grade (2.5); scope and decision log in every reviewer brief (1.5); the agreed safety net for security code (2.5); consensus is never the bar (1.5); decided items frozen (1.5); facts settled by the smallest test (1.5, 4); design for the current release only and time-boxed (2.1, 1.5); at most three review rounds, then one page to the owner (2.5).

The design phase just finished shows both sides: three-model rounds found real defects (a blocker no single review caught, JS-16), and round 3 still produced 8 MEDIUM findings, all about text written to fix round 2. The round cap and the owner's page stopped it.

## The nine guards (the gaps a three-model review would hit)

| # | Gap | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | Three copies of one question: duplicates, and "the higher grade stands" inflates every finding | **One lens per model, not three copies.** Fable: safety and trust (the agreed safety net, false PROVEN). Opus: does the change match the agreed design and the pull request's criteria. Sonnet: tests and evidence (each criterion has a test seen failing, coverage, every claim backed by a run). Each grades only inside its lens. |
| G2 | A finding can block without saying what agreed thing it breaks | **Every HIGH or MEDIUM must cite what it breaks:** one of the pull request's agreed criteria, a section of the agreed design, one of the release's three done lines, or a hole in the agreed safety net. The reviewer brief says a finding with no citation is PARKED by definition. The build session checks only that the citation exists and is real; it never re-grades. |
| G3 | Every round re-runs all three models, and each new pass finds new things | **Round 1:** all three, the whole pull request. **Rounds 2 and 3:** only the model whose finding is open re-checks its own findings and the lines changed to fix them; a new finding there counts only if HIGH. A confirm-only check is not a round. Round 3 still blocking: one page to the owner (as now). |
| G4 | No limit on volume: dozens of MEDIUMs, each blocking | **Volume stop:** more than 8 blocking findings in round 1 means the pull request is too big or the design has a gap. Fixing stops and the owner gets the list with two choices: split the pull request, or re-agree the design point. |
| G5 | "Fix every instance of the class" grows a pull request | **Fix the instance in scope.** Other instances go to known issues or parking, unless the class is itself one of the pull request's criteria. |
| G6 | Findings about old code become a stream of new stories | **Old code stays "Found, not fixed"** with the reviewer's grade (1.10). A HIGH on old code becomes a story only with the owner's yes and the budget re-agreed; at most one such story per release before "done" is re-agreed. |
| G7 | No ceiling on review spend | **Review budget for v0.4.0: $250, hard stop $300**, reported at 80% ($200), counted from each run's own cost record. |
| G8 | Divergence is noticed late | **Divergence stop:** if a round finds more blocking findings than the round before, fixing stops and the owner sees why. Every pull request records rounds, findings per round by grade, PARKED count, findings inside the premortem areas, and review cost (in the PR and `docs/hours.csv`). |
| G9 | A review turns into a redesign | Already a rule (2.2): no significant design change, not even a review's, without the owner's yes. Reviewers get the decision log and the owner's answers L1 to L9 as frozen. |

## Cost

Three models at extra effort cost about $20 to $25 for a first round; with G3, later rounds cost one model each (about $3 to $9). For 5 to 6 pull requests: about $130 to $200, inside G7's $250.
